Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information UX90 Creations collects through this site, why it is collected, and how it is used to respond to project inquiries and improve site performance.

Effective date: May 7, 2026
Contact: hello@ux90.dev

Information we collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, service interest, and message details submitted through the contact form.
  • Basic analytics and visit data used to understand how pages perform and where inquiries originate.
  • Communication records when a prospect contacts UX90 Creations through email or WhatsApp.

How we use information

  • To respond to project requests, proposals, support questions, and follow-up messages.
  • To improve website performance, service messaging, and conversion flows.
  • To maintain records of prospective and active client communications.

Data sharing and retention

  • UX90 Creations does not sell personal information.
  • Information may be processed by service providers used for contact handling, analytics, or email delivery.
  • Inquiry records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for sales, support, compliance, or bookkeeping purposes.

Your choices

  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry data by emailing hello@ux90.dev.
  • You can avoid submitting personal information by not using the contact form or direct messaging channels.
  • If analytics cookies are introduced in the future, this policy should be updated to reflect those controls.
